03 — DIFFERENT TYPES OF MEMORY

DRAM vs HBM

DRAM

RAM
  • Used in normal computers
  • Common in laptops and desktops
  • Available in DDR generations
  • Designed for general computing
  • Lower cost compared with HBM
VS

HBM

HBM
  • High Bandwidth Memory
  • Used heavily in AI hardware
  • Very high data bandwidth
  • Placed close to AI accelerators
  • More expensive and difficult to manufacture
