02 — THE AI CONNECTION

Why Does AI Need So Much Memory?

01

Large AI Models

Modern AI models can contain billions or even trillions of parameters. These parameters need memory while the model is being trained or used.

02

AI Data Centres

AI companies operate large data centres containing thousands of GPUs and servers. These systems require huge amounts of high-speed memory.

03

High-Speed Memory

AI workloads require memory that can move massive amounts of data very quickly. This has increased demand for technologies such as HBM.

04

Growing AI Usage

Chatbots, image generation, video generation and other AI applications are increasing the amount of computing infrastructure required around the world.
