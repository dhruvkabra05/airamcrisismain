Possible Effects Of The Memory Crunch

01

Higher Prices

Increased demand and limited manufacturing capacity can put pressure on memory prices.

02

Supply Pressure

Memory manufacturers have to balance demand from AI infrastructure with demand from traditional electronics.

03

Data Centre Growth

More AI services require more servers, accelerators and high-speed memory.

04

Consumer Hardware

Changes in the memory market can eventually affect computers, laptops and other electronic products.
